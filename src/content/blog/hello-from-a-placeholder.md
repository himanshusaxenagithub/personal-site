This is **example content**. Rewrite it, or delete the file and add your own posts under `src/content/blog/`.

Each post is Markdown with a small frontmatter schema (`title`, `description`, `pubDate`, optional `tags` and `draft`). The RSS feed at `/rss.xml` picks up published posts automatically.

A few things you might write about later:

- Notes from a paper you are reading
- A project write-up that does not belong on GitHub alone
- Short updates that do not need a full article

Set `draft: true` to keep a post out of the index and feed until it is ready.
